# Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2025-05-02

## Introduction

The no-more-mdash Chrome extension ("Extension") does not collect, transmit, or store any personal or identifiable user information. This Privacy Policy outlines the data practices of the Extension.

## Data Collection

- The Extension operates solely on the client side and modifies text content within the user's browser.  
- No data is sent to external servers.  
- No analytics, crash reporting, or logging is performed.

## Third-Party Links

This Extension does not include third-party libraries or resources.

## Updates to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted with a revised Effective Date.
