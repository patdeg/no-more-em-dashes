# Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2025-05-02

## Introduction

The ChatGPT: no more em-dashes Chrome extension ("Extension") does not collect, transmit, or store any personal or identifiable user information. This Privacy Policy outlines the data practices of the Extension.

- The Extension operates solely on the client side and modifies text content within the user's browser.  
- No data is sent to external servers.  
- No analytics, crash reporting, or logging is performed.
